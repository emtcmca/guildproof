## Rewrite

Our platform makes your team's workflow better.

## Tone targeted

Warm, plain. Second person ("your team"), short words, no jargon.

## What changed and why

- **Filler and hype cut.** I removed "cutting-edge," "robust," "leverages synergy," "seamlessly," "revolutionize," "basically," and "just." None of them said anything checkable.
- **The point moved to the front.** The source buried its only claim after five buzzwords. The claim is that the platform makes your team's workflow better, so the rewrite opens with it.
- **"Everything" dropped.** "Make everything better" is an unsupportable sweep. "Your team's workflow" is the only thing the source actually names.
- **Shorter, not just cheaper.** The copy went from 24 words to 8. It has nothing to add, so nothing was padded back in.

## Flags

- **The source has almost no content.** It never says what the platform is, what it does, or what "better" means (faster, cheaper, fewer errors). I kept the rewrite equally vague and did not invent specifics.
- **This is a placeholder, not finished copy.** To make it warm and persuasive, I need one concrete fact, such as what the platform does or one measurable result. I can then rewrite around that.
- **No claims were added.** Everything in the rewrite was in the source: a platform, a team, a workflow, "better." "Revolutionize" was hype rather than a fact, so I dropped it.
